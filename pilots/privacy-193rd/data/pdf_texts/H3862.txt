An Act providing for the terms of certain bonds to provide for a program of housing, community 
development, economic opportunities, support for local governments, increased innovation and 
job creation to be issued by the Commonwealth. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
facilitate forthwith the issuance of certain bonds to provide for a program of housing, community 
development, economic opportunities, support for local governments, increased innovation and 
job creation, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate 
preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:
