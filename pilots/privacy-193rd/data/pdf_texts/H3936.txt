To the Honorable Senate and House of Representatives, 
 
 I am filing for your prompt consideration a bill entitled “An Act Making Certain 
Appropriations for Fiscal Year 2024 Before Final Action on the General Appropriation Bill.” 
 Section 1 provides the spending authorization to maintain necessary services through July 
31, 2023, pending the passage of the General Appropriation Act. All actions taken under this 
section will apply to the General Appropriation Act. Section 2 allows advance local aid 
payments to be made to any city, town, regional school district, or independent agricultural and 
technical school that demonstrates an emergency cash shortfall. Section 3 extends capital 
accounts that would otherwise expire. 
 In order to ensure that the Commonwealth will be able to meet its payment obligations 
without delays, I urge your favorable action on this bill no later than June 26, 2023. 
 Respectfully submitted, 
 
 Maura T. Healey, 
 Governor
