To the Honorable Senate and House of Representatives, 
 
 Pursuant to the provisions of Article II, Section 8, Paragraph 1, Clause (2) of the 
Amendments to the Constitution, as appearing in Article LXXXIX of said Amendments, I am 
filing for your consideration the attached legislation entitled “An Act Validating the Results of 
the Annual Election Held in the Town of Deerfield on May 2, 2022.” 
 I am filing this special legislation at the request of the Town of Deerfield’s Selectboard. 
 The legislation ratifies all acts and proceedings taken by the Town at its annual town 
election held on May 2, 2022. The ratification serves to resolve any question that might arise 
from the fact that the warrant for the election inadvertently omitted the text of a Proposition 2 ½ 
ballot question which passed by a wide margin. Through that ballot question, the voters of 
Deerfield approved a debt exclusion for a $3 million bond to pay for costs of upgrading the 
South Deerfield Wastewater Treatment Facility. Major upgrades to that facility are already 
underway, and the bond is intended to support the second phase of what is anticipated to be a 
four-phase project. 
 Although the warrant for the election inadvertently omitted the text of the ballot question, 
the full text of the question was properly printed on the ballot and posted at least 7 days prior to 
the election. Timely ratification of the results of the election is necessary to permit the Town to 
proceed with issuing the bond and continuing with this important public works project. I 
therefore urge your prompt consideration of this bill. 
 Respectfully submitted, 
 
 
 Maura T. Healey 
 Governor

An Act validating the results of the annual election held in the town of Deerfield on May 2, 
2022. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. Notwithstanding any general or special law or town bylaw or charter 
2 provision to the contrary, all acts and proceedings taken by the town of Deerfield at the annual 
3 town election held on May 2, 2022, and all actions taken pursuant thereto, are hereby ratified, 
4 validated and confirmed to the same extent as if the town had complied with all statutory 
5 requirements for posting a warrant for the meeting. 
6 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
