10 Section 2AAAAAA. (a) There shall be established and set up on the books of the

16 earned on any amounts deposited in the fund. Amounts credited to the fund shall be expended

18 shall remain available for expenditure in subsequent fiscal years. No expenditure made from the 
19 fund shall cause the fund to be in deficit at any point. 
20 (b) Quarterly, the Comptroller shall transfer interest credited to the Stabilization Fund 
21 during the previous quarter to the fund.

25 and means the balance of the Stabilization Fund as of June 30 of the preceding fiscal year as well

28 recently ended fiscal year has increased over the balance of the Stabilization Fund at the close of
