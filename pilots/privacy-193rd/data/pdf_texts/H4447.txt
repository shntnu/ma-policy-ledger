An Act authorizing the Division of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance to convey a 
certain portion of the Gardner Heritage State Park to the city of Gardner. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
provide for environmental remediation for the productive use of a certain parcel of land in the 
city of Gardner, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the 
immediate preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. Section 2 of chapter 147 of the acts of 2020 is hereby amended by striking 
2 out the words “for general municipal purposes,”. 
3 SECTION 2. Said section 2 of said chapter 147 is hereby further amended by inserting 
4 after first sentence the following sentence:- The parcels may be used by the city of Gardner for 
5 general municipal purposes or may be conveyed by the city of Gardner to a developer for 
6 redevelopment. 
7 SECTION 3. Section 5 of said chapter 147 is hereby amended by striking out the first 
8 sentence and inserting in place thereof the following 2 sentences:- 
3 of 3 
9 The commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance may grant a credit to the 
10 city of Gardner for estimated environmental remediation costs for the parcels identified in 
11 section 2; provided, however, that if the total value of said credit and the appraised value of the 
12 parcel identified in section 1 exceed the appraised value of the parcels identified in section 2, the 
13 commonwealth shall have no obligation to pay the difference to the city of Gardner. If the 
14 appraised value of the parcels identified in section 2 exceed the appraised value of the parcel 
15 identified in section 1, the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance, on 
16 behalf of the department of conservation and recreation, shall, as consideration for the excess 
17 value of the parcels described in said section 2, also acquire from the city of Gardner payment in 
18 an amount equal to the difference in the appraised values through the transfer to the department 
19 of conservation and recreation of land or an interest in land or funding for the acquisition of land 
20 or an interest therein equal to or greater than the resource value difference in the appraised 
21 values.
