An Act financing improvements to municipal roads and bridges. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
finance improvements to the commonwealth’s transportation system, therefore it is hereby 
declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public 
convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:

55 SECTION 11. To meet the expenditures necessary in carrying out section 2, the state 
56 treasurer shall, upon request of the governor, issue and sell bonds of the commonwealth in an 
57 amount to be specified by the governor from time to time but not exceeding, in the aggregate, 
58 $225,000,000. All bonds issued by the commonwealth as aforesaid shall be designated on their 
59 face, Commonwealth Transportation Improvement Act of 2024, and shall be issued for a 
60 maximum term of years, not exceeding 30 years, as the governor may recommend to the general 
61 court under section 3 of Article LXII of the Amendments to the Constitution. All such bonds 
62 shall be payable not later than June 30, 2059, pursuant to said section 3 of said Article LXII. All 
63 interest and payments on account of principal on these obligations shall be payable from the 
64 General Fund or the Commonwealth Transportation Fund. Notwithstanding any other general or 
65 special law to the contrary, bonds issued under this section and interest thereon shall be general 
66 obligations of the commonwealth.
