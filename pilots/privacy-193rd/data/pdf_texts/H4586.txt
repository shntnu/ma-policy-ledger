HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 8, 2024. 
 The committee on Education, to whom were referred the joint petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 609) of Thomas P. Walsh, Joan B. 
Lovely and James K. Hawkins relative to virtual school eligibility for 
school choice students; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House No. 
4119) of Jeffrey N. Roy that the Executive Office of Education establish 
an adult workforce diploma program to assist certain adults in obtaining 
high school diplomas and developing employability and career technical 
skills, reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 
4586) ought to be adopted. 
 
 For the committee, 
 
DENISE C. GARLICK.
