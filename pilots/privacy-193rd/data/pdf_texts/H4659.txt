An Act relative to the compulsory automobile insurance limits. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. Section 34O of Chapter 90 of the General Laws, as so appearing in the 2020 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out in line 17 the word “five” and inserting in 
3 place thereof the word “twenty-five”. 
4 SECTION 2. The commissioner of insurance is hereby directed to undertake a study, in 
5 consultation with representatives of the property and casualty industry and of the independent 
6 insurance agents, of the adequacy of the Commonwealth’s mandatory minimum policy limits for 
7 bodily injury and property damage motor vehicle liability and shall make recommendations on 
8 how best to update said limits in a way that minimizes the impact on auto insurance rates in the 
9 Commonwealth. Said study shall be undertaken every ten years. The study shall consider: 
10 (i) the adequacy of the current limits to cover the costs of bodily injury and property 
11 claims; 
12 (ii) the impact of increasing the current limits on auto premiums; and
