An Act honoring, empowering, and recognizing our servicemembers and veterans. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
provide forthwith to honor, empower and recognize servicemembers and veterans in the 
commonwealth, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the 
immediate preservation of the public convenience.
