An Act relative to town meeting vacancy in the town of Burlington. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. Chapter 686 of the acts of 1970, as amended by section 8 of chapter 160 of 
2 the acts of 1993, is hereby further amended by striking out section 9 and inserting in place 
3 thereof the following section:- 
4 Section 9. (a) The office of a town meeting member shall become vacant upon their 
5 death, resignation or removal from office in any manner authorized by law. 
6 (b) A vacancy in the town meeting membership shall be filled for the remainder of the 
7 unexpired term, if any, at the next regular town election if such vacancy is by death, resignation 
8 or removal from office in any manner authorized by law. 
9 (c) A vacancy in the town meeting membership shall be filled for the remainder of the 
10 unexpired term, if any, at the next regular town election. When a vacancy in the town meeting 
11 membership occurs and no town election is to be held within 60 days of such vacancy, the 
12 moderator shall direct the town clerk to post notice of any such vacancy and a request for 
2 of 2 
13 candidates. Said notice shall be published by the town clerk not less than 14 days prior to the 
14 next scheduled town meeting: (i) in a newspaper; (ii) on the bulletin board outside the town 
15 clerk’s office; and (iii) on the town’s website. 
16 (d) All candidate information for the town meeting membership received by the town 
17 clerk shall be forwarded to the remaining members prior to the vote; said vote to take place at the 
18 next scheduled town meeting. The vacancy shall be filled by a majority vote of the remaining 
19 precinct members in attendance at the next scheduled town meeting. 
20 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
