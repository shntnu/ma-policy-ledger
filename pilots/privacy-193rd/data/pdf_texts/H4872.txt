HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, 
 The committee on Children, Families and Persons with Disabilities, to 
whom were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 128) of 
Christine P. Barber and others relative to ensuring equal access to medical 
treatments essential for people with a developmental disability, intellectual 
disability, or autism; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 158) of 
Tricia Farley-Bouvier and others relative to establishing the Massachusetts 
foster care review office; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, 
No. 191) of Kay Khan and others relative to a livable wage for human 
services workers, reports recommending that the accompanying order 
(House, No. 4872) ought to be adopted.
