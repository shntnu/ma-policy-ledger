HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 24, 2024. 
 The committee on Public Safety and Homeland Security, to whom 
were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2315) of 
David F. DeCoste, Colleen M. Garry and Michael J. Soter for legislation 
to further define hate crimes; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, 
No. 2336) of Danielle W. Gregoire and Carole A. Fiola relative to the 
expansion of the state DNA database; the petition (accompanied by bill, 
House, No. 2344) of Russell E. Holmes relative to police transparency; the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2348) of Mary S. Keefe and 
others relative to military grade controlled property; the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 2407) of Jeffrey Rosario Turco relative 
to the membership of the Peace Officer Standards and Training 
Commission; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2412) of 
Erika Uyterhoeven for legislation to increase oversight of the 
Massachusetts State Police through stringent budget accounting and 
regulation of overtime reporting, reports recommending that the 
accompanying order (House, No. 4878) ought to be adopted [Senator 
Velis dissents]. 
 
For the committee, 
 
CARLOS GONZÁLEZ.
