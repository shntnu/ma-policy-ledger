HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, July 24, 2024. 
 The committee on Financial Services, to whom were referred the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 955) of Daniel Cahill relative to 
banking laws and related statutes; the petition (accompanied by bill, 
House, No. 975) of Mike Connolly, Antonio F. D. Cabral and others for 
legislation to establish a Massachusetts public bank; the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 1005) of Michael J. Finn relative to the 
licensure of motor vehicle damage appraisers; the petition (accompanied 
by bill, House, No. 1018) of Sean Garballey for legislation to further 
regulate the rental of motor vehicles; the petition (accompanied by bill, 
House, No. 1023) of Carmine Lawrence Gentile and Samantha Montaño 
relative to economic mobility through matched savings; and the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3583) of Bruce J. Ayers relative to 
customer reimbursement in instances of financial fraud, reports 
recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 4898) ought to 
be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
JAMES M. MURPHY.
