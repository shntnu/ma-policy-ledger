HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, August 15, 2024. 
 The committee on Public Safety and Homeland Security, to whom 
were referred the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2378) of Paul 
McMurtry and Patrick Joseph Kearney relative to the Massachusetts peace 
officer standards and training commission; the petition (accompanied by 
bill, House, No. 2394) of David M. Rogers, Marjorie C. Decker and others 
relative to the right of persons incarcerated in correctional institutions, 
state prisons, jails or houses of correction to confer with news media 
representative; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 2408) of 
Jeffrey Rosario Turco relative to the membership of the Peace Officer 
Standards and Training Commission; the petition (accompanied by bill, 
House, No. 2409) of Jeffrey Rosario Turco that law enforcement 
departments provide resources to ensure officers can make accurate 
statements; and the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3863) of 
Susan Williams Gifford and Joseph D. McKenna relative to the 
maintenance of records on the transfer of firearms, rifles, shotguns, 
machine guns and ammunition, reports recommending that the 
accompanying order (House, No. 4902) ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
CARLOS GONZÁLEZ. 
 
 
 
 
 
HOUSE . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . No. 4902 
 
 
The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
 
 
 
 
House of Representatives, August 15, 2024. 
 
 Ordered, That the committee on Public Safety and Homeland Security be authorized to 
sit during a recess of the General Court to make an investigation and study of House documents 
numbered 2378, 2394, 2408, 2409 and 3863, relative to Peace Officer Standards and Training 
Commission, incarcerated persons and other public safety and homeland security issues 
 Said committee shall report to the General Court the results of its investigation and study 
and its recommendations, if any, together with drafts of legislation necessary to carry such 
recommendations into effect, by filing the same with the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
on or before December 31, 2024.
