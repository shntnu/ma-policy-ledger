Text of a further amendment, offered by Mr. Walsh of Peabody, to the Senate amendments of the 
House Bill authorizing the town of Canton to grant temporary and permanent easements over 
certain conservation land situated in the town of Stoughton for highway purposes (House, No. 
3937, amended). July 30, 2024.

 By striking out all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the following:- 
1 SECTION 1. Pursuant to chapter 3 § 5A of the General Laws, notwithstanding the 
2 provisions of any other general or special law to the contrary, the conservation commission of 
3 the town of Canton may transfer to the select board of the town of Canton, for general municipal 
4 purposes, certain portions of land owned by the town of Canton under the care, custody and 
5 control of the conservation commission for conservation purposes and situated in the town of 
6 Stoughton, said portions being identified as permanent easements on the Plan entitled 
7 “Massachusetts Department of Transportation Highway Division Plan and Profile of Washington 
8 Street (Route 138) in the Town of Stoughton, Norfolk County, Accepted Preliminary Right of 
9 Way Plans” and being a portion of those parcels described in the assessor’s records of the town 
10 of Stoughton as Map 57, Parcel 91 and Map 58, Parcel 1. 
11 SECTION 2. Pursuant to chapter 3 § 5A of the General Laws, notwithstanding the 
12 provisions of any other general or special law to the contrary, the board of water commissioners 
13 of the town of Canton may transfer to the select board of the town of Canton for general 
2 of 3 
14 municipal purposes, certain portions of land owned by the town of Canton under the care, 
15 custody and control of the board of water commissioners for water supply purposes, situated in 
16 the town of Stoughton and identified as permanent easements on the Plan entitled 
17 “Massachusetts Department of Transportation Highway Division Plan and Profile of Washington 
18 Street (Route 138) in the Town of Stoughton, Norfolk County, Accepted Preliminary Right of 
19 Way Plans” being a portion of those parcels described in the assessor’s records of the town of 
20 Stoughton as Map 57, Parcels 92 and 93. 
21 SECTION 3. The select board of the town of Canton may grant certain permanent 
22 easements on the land identified in section 1 and section 2, to the commonwealth for highway 
23 purposes, to support the roadway improvements project being undertaken by the commonwealth 
24 for Route 138. 
25 SECTION 4. As mitigation for the transfers described in sections 1, 2 and 3, the 
26 Massachusetts Department of Transportation shall provide funding in lieu of replacement land to 
27 the town of Canton, which shall be not less than 110 per cent of the fair market value or value in 
28 use of the impacted lands, whichever is greater, as determined by an independent appraisal. The 
29 town of Canton shall deposit such sum into its Conservation Law Fund and dedicate it solely for 
30 the acquisition of land for conservation or water supply purposes. The town of Canton shall use 
31 such funds within 3 years to acquire replacement land, which is in a comparable location, of 
32 equal or greater natural resource value as determined by the secretary of energy and 
33 environmental affairs, and of equal or greater acreage and monetary value, as determined by an 
34 independent appraisal of the fair market value or value in use, whichever is greater, of the 
35 impacted lands.
