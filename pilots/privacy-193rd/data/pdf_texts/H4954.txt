_______________ 
 
An Act designating a certain overpass in the city of New Bedford as the Andre Lopes Korean 
War Veteran Overpass. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. The pedestrian overpass spanning route 18 in the city of New Bedford, 
2 constructed pursuant to MBTA contract number K78CN06 as part of the South Coast Rail 
3 project, in the vicinity of 618 Acushnet Avenue, shall be designated and known as the Andre 
4 Lopes Korean War Veteran Overpass, in memory of Andre Lopes, who died as a result of 
5 injuries incurred while a member of the armed forces of the United States during the Korean 
6 Conflict. The Massachusetts Department of Transportation or its successor shall erect and 
7 maintain suitable markers bearing that designation in compliance with the standards of the 
8 department. 
9 SECTION 2. This act shall take effect upon its passage.
