An Act authorizing the commissioner of Capital Asset Management and Maintenance to convey 
a certain parcel of land in the town of Tewksbury. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
authorize forthwith the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance to convey a 
certain parcel of land in the town of Tewksbury, therefore it is hereby declared to be an 
emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. (a) Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the 
2 General Laws, or any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of capital 
3 asset management and maintenance may convey to the town of Tewksbury, for use as a 
4 cemetery, a certain parcel of land being approximately 11.019 acres previously conveyed to the 
5 department of public health by deed recorded November 23, 1979 at the Middlesex north registry 
6 of deeds in book 2396, page 686 and shown as “parcel 62-19-3” on a plan of land on file with the 
7 division of capital asset management and maintenance entitled “Approval not Required Plan in 
8 the Town of Tewksbury, Massachusetts Middlesex County Owned by Commonwealth of 
9 Massachusetts Prepared for the Division of Capital Asset Management On Behalf of the 
2 of 3 
10 Department of Conversation and Recreation”, dated September 12, 2012 and prepared by Nitsch 
11 Engineering, Inc. 
12 (b) Prior to finalizing the transaction or making the conveyance authorized in subsection 
13 (a), the division of capital asset management and maintenance may make minor modifications to 
14 the area and plan in order to carry out the purposes of this section. The commissioner of capital 
15 asset management and maintenance shall establish the fair market value of the property for both 
16 the highest and best use of the property as currently encumbered and for the purposes described

18 (c) Consideration for the parcel described in subsection (a) shall be fair market value of

22 between the calculated value and the price received.
