 Text of an amendment, recommended by the committee on Ways and Means, to the Senate

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts 
 
_______________ 
In the One Hundred and Ninety-Third General Court 
(2023-2024) 
_______________ 
 
 By striking out all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the following:– 
 
1 (a) Pursuant to chapter 65 of the acts of 2010 and notwithstanding section 5A of chapter 3 
2 of the General Laws or any other general or special law to the contrary, the commissioner of 
3 capital asset management and maintenance, in consultation with the commissioner of 
4 conservation and recreation, may lease or enter into other agreements with Riverside Boat Club 
5 of Cambridge in the city of Cambridge, for a reconfigured parcel of land for the continued 
6 operation of its historical rowing program and boathouse. The parcel of land is more particularly 
7 described on a plan of land, on file with the department of conservation and recreation, entitled 
8 “Plan Showing Reoriented Boundary for Riverside Boat Club of Cambridge”, revised July 29, 
9 2024, including adjacent water sheet currently being used and shall include appurtenant rights of 
10 access from adjoining public roadways. 
11 (b) The commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance may: (i) make minor 
12 revisions to the plan pursuant to paragraph (a) and shall file the final plan with the Middlesex 
13 southern district registry of deeds; and (ii) execute any leases or other agreements consistent with 
14 said chapter 65.
