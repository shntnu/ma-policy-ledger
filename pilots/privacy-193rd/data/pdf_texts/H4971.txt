_______________ 
 
 By striking out all after the enacting clause and inserting in place thereof the following:– 
 
1 SECTION 1. Section 1 of chapter 314 of the acts of 1993 is hereby amended by striking 
2 out the words “the expansion of the town of Tewksbury baseball facilities” and inserting in place 
3 thereof the following words:- general recreational purposes, including, but not limited to, 
4 Tewksbury baseball facilities and other active and passive recreational activities. 
5 SECTION 2. Notwithstanding sections 32 to 37, inclusive, of chapter 7C of the General 
6 Laws, section 2 of chapter 314 of the acts of 1993 or any other general or special law to the 
7 contrary, the commissioner of capital asset management and maintenance may amend the 
8 restriction in a deed recorded in the Middlesex northern district registry of deeds in book 10054, 
9 page 109 to allow the parcel of land described therein to be used for general recreational 
10 purposes. 
11 SECTION 3. The town of Tewksbury shall be responsible for all costs and expenses of 
12 any transaction authorized in this act as determined by the commissioner of capital asset 
13 management and maintenance, including, but not limited to, the costs of any recording fees or 
14 deed preparation.
