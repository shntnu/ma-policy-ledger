An Act authorizing project labor agreements. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 (a) Notwithstanding section 39M of chapter 30 of the General Laws, chapter 149 of the 
2 General Laws and chapter 149A of the General Laws, a public agency or municipality may 
3 require a project labor agreement on contracts for public works construction and may require the 
4 project labor agreement to be incorporated into the contract specifications; provided, that prior to 
5 including a project labor agreement requirement, the public agency or municipality shall make a 
6 determination prior to issuing a request for proposals or bids that the project labor agreement on 
7 a specific project is in the best interest of the commonwealth, public agency or municipality. 
8 (b) In making a determination of the best interest of the commonwealth, public agency or 
9 municipality pursuant to subsection (a), the agency or municipality shall consider the effects a 
10 project labor agreement may have on: (i) construction efficiency, cost and direct and indirect 
11 economic benefits to the public agency or municipality; (ii) the availability of a sufficient supply 
12 of skilled, qualified workers to complete the project; (iii) the timing of, and the prevention of 
13 delays or disruptions to, the construction process; (iv) the safety and quality of the public 
14 construction project; (v) the expansion of apprenticeship programs and workforce development 
2 of 2 
15 in the construction industry; and (vi) the promotion of employment and training opportunities for 
16 women, minority workers and veterans. 
17 (c) The department of labor standards shall promulgate regulations to increase diversity 
18 of contractors in project labor agreements, including, but not limited to: (i) incentivizing a certain 
19 percentage of contracts with minority-owned businesses; and (ii) demographics of the workforce 
20 reflecting the demographics, to the extent possible, where a project is located.
