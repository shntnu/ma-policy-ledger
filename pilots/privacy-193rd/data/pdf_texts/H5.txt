Dear Mr. James: 
 
In compliance with Section 33 of Chapter 30 of the Massachusetts General Laws, as 
Inspector General, I respectfully submit two legislative proposals for the 2023-2024 session of the 
General Court. 
 
1. The first proposal, An Act Relative to the Consolidation of Inspector General Annual 
Reports, would eliminate the requirement that two embedded units in the Office – the 
Internal Special Audit Unit and the Division of State Police Oversight – submit separate 
annual reports to the Legislature. Instead, these repo rts would be included as part of the 
Office’s annual report required under Section 12 of Chapter 12A of the Massachusetts 
General Laws. The Office would now be required to submit its annual report to both the 
Joint Committee on Transportation and the Joint Committee on Public Safety and 
Homeland Security, as the Internal Special Audit Unit and the Division of State Police 
Oversight currently submit their annual reports to these committees. 
 
2. The second proposal, An Act Relative to Technical Corrections in Ch apter 30B, would 
update Chapter 30B of the Massachusetts General Laws to include correct statutory 
references based on amendments to other statutes. The proposal also strikes a section of 
Chapter 30B that is duplicative. 
 
Thank you for your attention to this matter. Please do not hesitate to contact Joshua Giles, 
Director of the Policy and Government Division, at Joshua.Giles@mass.gov or me, 
Jeffrey.S.Shapiro@mass.gov, if you have any questions.
