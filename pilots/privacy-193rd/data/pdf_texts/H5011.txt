HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, August 29, 2024. 
 The committee on Education, to whom were referred the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 438) of Antonio F. D. Cabral and others 
relative to competency determination and high school diplomas; the 
petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 488) of Carlos González, Bud 
L. Williams and Vanna Howard for legislation to eliminate the use of the 
Massachusetts Comprehensive Assessment System Test for high school 
graduation; the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 495) of James 
K. Hawkins, Samantha Montaño and others relative to the comprehensive 
support and improvement of schools; and the petition (accompanied by 
bill, House, No. 600) of Andres X. Vargas, Manny Cruz and others for 
legislation to establish a special commission (including members of the 
General Court) to be known as the promise commission to conduct an 
ongoing study of all matters concerning education in the 21st century, 
reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, No. 5011) 
ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
ANDRES X. VARGAS.
