HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, September 12, 2024. 
 The committee on Health Care Financing, to whom were referred the 
Bill to ensure access to medical parole (House, No. 2319); the Bill relative 
to health insurance and incarcerated individuals (House, No. 2327); the 
Bill relative to health education in women's correctional institutions 
(House, No. 2349); the Bill to ensure compliance with the anti-shackling 
law for pregnant incarcerated women (House, No. 2350); the Bill to 
provide reproductive health care to incarcerated individuals (House, No. 
2371); the Bill to remove medical and health service fees for incarcerated 
people (House, No. 2372); the Bill relative to the postpartum treatment of 
women in correctional facilities (House, No. 2404); the Bill relative to 
overdose mapping (House, No. 4654); and the Bill relative to non-opioid 
alternatives in pain treatment (House, No. 4656), reports recommending 
that the accompanying order (House, No. 5016) ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee,
