HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES, August 29, 2024. 
 The committee on Transportation, to whom were referred the petition 
(accompanied by bill, House, No. 3288) of Rob Consalvo and others that 
the Massachusetts Bay Transportation Authority designate all commuter 
rail stations in the city of Boston as Zone 1A to promote fare equity; and 
the petition (accompanied by bill, House, No. 3289) of Rob Consalvo 
relative to access to certain data by private detectives and private 
investigators, reports recommending that the accompanying order (House, 
No. 5022) ought to be adopted. 
 
For the committee, 
 
WILLIAM M. STRAUS.
