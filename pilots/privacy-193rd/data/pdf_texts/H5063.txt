An Act relative to designation of the state public health laboratory campus in honor of Dr. Alfred 
DeMaria, Jr. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
designate the campus of the State Laboratory Institute, also known as the Massachusetts State 
Public Health Laboratory Campus, as the Dr. Alfred DeMaria, Jr. Campus in honor of Dr. Alfred 
DeMaria, Jr., therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate 
preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 The campus of the State Laboratory Institute, also known as the Massachusetts State 
2 Public Health Laboratory Campus, located in the Jamaica Plain section of the city of Boston, 
3 shall be designated and known as the Dr. Alfred DeMaria, Jr. Campus, in recognition of Dr. 
4 Alfred DeMaria, Jr., a pioneer in epidemiology and former state epidemiologist, chief infectious 
5 disease medical officer and assistant commissioner of the department of public health. A suitable 
6 marker bearing that designation shall be attached to the campus and maintained by the 
7 department of public health. Nothing in this section shall be construed to modify the designation 
8 of the tower building on said campus as the Dr. William A. Hinton Laboratory pursuant to 
9 chapter 124 of the acts of 2009.
