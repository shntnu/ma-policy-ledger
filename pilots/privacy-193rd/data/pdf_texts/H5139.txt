An Act relative to commercial driver's licenses. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
amend the lookback period for certain disqualifying offenses for commercial driver’s licenses, 
therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, necessary for the immediate preservation 
of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 SECTION 1. Section 9 of chapter 90F of the General Laws, as appearing in the 2022 
2 Official Edition, is hereby amended by striking out, in line 21, the word “subsection” and 
3 inserting in place thereof the following word:- paragraph. 
4 SECTION 2. Said section 9 of said chapter 90F, as so appearing, is hereby further 
5 amended by striking out, in line 96, the word “subsection” and inserting in place thereof the
