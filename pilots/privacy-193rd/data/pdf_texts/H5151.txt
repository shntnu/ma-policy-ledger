An Act relative to the transportation of vessels. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows: 
 
1 Notwithstanding any general or special law, rule or regulation to the contrary, the 
2 maximum height and width dimensions as provided in sections 19 or 19A of chapter 90 of the 
3 General Laws shall not apply to any vessel being hauled from or to a professional waterfront 
4 year-round marine servicing business in the village of Hyannis or Osterville on a public way 
5 within 3 miles of the vessel’s waterfront location of origin in the town of Barnstable and which 
6 may pass over the roadways of the town of Yarmouth; provided, that said vessel shall be trucked 
7 by a professional licensed boat hauler.
