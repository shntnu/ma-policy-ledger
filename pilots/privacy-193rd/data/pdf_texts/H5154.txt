13 CHAPTER 90K. AUTOMATED ENFORCEMENT OF BUS STOPS AND BUS 
14 LANES 
15 Section 1. As used in this chapter, the following words shall, unless the context clearly 
16 requires otherwise, have the following meanings: 
17 “Authority”, as defined in section 1 of chapter 161A. 
18 “Bus mounted camera system”, a motor vehicle sensor device, which produces digital 
19 photographs or video recordings of a motor vehicle that commits a camera enforceable violation, 
20 installed on a bus owned or operated by the authority or by a regional transit authority 
21 established pursuant to chapter 161B that adopts this chapter; provided, however, that the device 
22 may be automated or operated manually. 
23 “Bus stop camera system”, a motor vehicle sensor device, which produces digital 
24 photographs or video recordings of a motor vehicle that commits a camera enforceable violation, 
25 installed in proximity to a posted bus stop by an enforcing authority. 
26 “Camera enforceable violation”, (i) parking in a posted bus stop; or (ii) parking, standing 
27 or causing a motor vehicle to stand in a lane designated for the exclusive use of buses unless 
28 otherwise regulated or posted by an official traffic signal, sign or marking or at the direction of 
29 an authorized police officer in violation of section 4E of chapter 89. 
30 “Enforcing authority”, the authority or a regional transit authority established pursuant to 
31 chapter 161B that adopts this chapter; provided, however, that a city or town served by the 
32 authority or the regional transit authority shall be the enforcing authority for bus stop camera 
33 systems. 
 
4 of 12 
34 “Registrar”, the registrar of motor vehicles. 
35 Section 2. (a) The authority and any regional transit authority established pursuant to 
36 chapter 161B that adopts this chapter may deploy bus mounted camera systems to enforce the 
37 priority of buses on roadways in lanes designated for the exclusive use of buses and bus stop 
38 violations pursuant to this chapter; provided, however, that an enforcing authority may deploy 
39 bus stop camera systems to enforce bus stop violations. 
40 (b) Fines imposed pursuant to this chapter shall be: (i) not less than $25 and not more 
41 than $125 for a camera enforceable violation involving parking, standing or causing a motor 
42 vehicle to stand in a lane designated for the exclusive use of buses, as established by the 
43 authority; provided, however, the authority shall establish separate fines for such violations for 
44 passenger and commercial vehicles; and (ii) $100 for a camera enforceable violation involving 
45 parking a motor vehicle in a posted bus stop. 
46 (c) The registered owner of a motor vehicle shall be liable for any fine unless the: 
47 (i) violation was necessary to allow the passage of an emergency vehicle; 
48 (ii) violation was incurred while participating in a funeral procession; 
49 (iii) violation was incurred during a period of time in which the motor vehicle was 
50 reported to the police department of any state, city or town as having been stolen and had not 
51 been recovered before the time the violation occurred; 
52 (iv) operator of the motor vehicle was operating the motor vehicle under a rental or lease 
53 agreement and the registered owner of the motor vehicle is a rental or leasing company and has 
54 complied with section 3; 
 
5 of 12 
55 (v) operator of the motor vehicle was issued a citation for the violation in accordance 
56 with section 2 of chapter 90C; 
57 (vi) violation was necessary for the safety of passengers or pedestrians; or 
58 (vii) violation was necessary to comply with any other law or regulation governing the 
59 operation of a motor vehicle. 
60 (d) An enforcing authority may send a written warning to the registered owner of a motor 
61 vehicle in lieu of enforcement through a fine; provided, however, that a written warning shall 
62 only be issued pursuant to objective and consistent criteria in a written policy established by the 
63 enforcing authority; provided further, that the authority shall provide guidance for establishing 
64 such objective and consistent criteria. 
65 (e) A certificate, or other written attestation, based upon inspection of photographs and 
66 data produced by a bus mounted or bus stop camera system and sworn to or affirmed by a 
67 representative of the enforcing authority shall be prima facie evidence of the facts contained 
68 therein. 
69 (f) A camera enforceable violation issued pursuant to this chapter shall not be: (i) made 
70 part of the driving record of the person upon whom such liability is imposed; or (ii) a conviction 
71 of a moving violation of the motor vehicle laws for the purpose of determining a surcharge on a 
72 motor vehicle premium pursuant to section 113B of chapter 175. 
73 (g) An enforcing authority that deploys a camera system pursuant to this chapter may hire 
74 and designate personnel as necessary or contract for services to implement this chapter. 
75 Notwithstanding any general or special law to the contrary, personnel designated to review and

76 issue citations pursuant to this chapter may be civilians and shall not be required to be a member 
77 of any police force. 
78 (h)(1) Subject to regulations promulgated by the authority, the enforcing authority shall 
79 provide a notice of violation to the registered owner of a motor vehicle that is identified in 
80 photographs or recordings produced by a bus mounted or bus stop camera system as evidence of 
81 a camera enforceable violation pursuant to this chapter; provided, however, that the notice shall 
82 include, but not be limited to: (i) a copy of the photographs produced by the camera system 
83 showing the vehicle in the process of a camera enforceable violation; (ii) the registration number 
84 and state of issuance of the vehicle; (iii) the date, time and location of the alleged camera 
85 enforceable violation; (iv) the specific camera enforceable violation charged; (v) instructions for 
86 payment of the fine imposed pursuant to subsection (b); (vi) instructions on how to appeal the 
87 camera enforceable violation in writing and to obtain a hearing; and (vii) an affidavit form 
88 approved by the enforcing authority for the purposes of making a written appeal pursuant to 
89 subsection (i). 
90 (2) In the case of a violation involving a motor vehicle registered in the commonwealth, 
91 the notice of violation shall be mailed to the address of the registered owner of the motor vehicle 
92 as listed in the records of the registrar. If a motor vehicle is registered under the laws of another 
93 state or county, the notice of violation shall be mailed to the address of the registered owner as 
94 listed in the records of the official in the state or county that has charge of the registration of the 
95 motor vehicle. If the address is unavailable, it shall be sufficient for the enforcing authority to 
96 mail a notice of violation to the official in the state or county that has charge of the registration 
97 of the motor vehicle.

98 (i)(1) A registered owner of a motor vehicle to whom a notice of violation has been 
99 issued pursuant to this chapter shall pay the fine or contest responsibility for the violation in a 
100 manner prescribed by the authority; provided, however, that the authority shall allow a fine to be 
101 contested in writing by mail or online. Payment of the established fine shall operate as the final 
102 disposition of a camera enforceable violation; provided, however, that payment by a registered 
103 owner of a motor vehicle shall operate as the final disposition of the violation as to any other 
104 registered owner of the same motor vehicle for the same violation. 
105 (2) Not more than 60 days after a camera enforceable violation under this chapter, a 
106 registered owner of a motor vehicle may contest responsibility for the violation in writing by 
107 mail or online. The registered owner shall provide the enforcing authority with a signed affidavit, 
108 in a form approved by the enforcing authority, stating the: (i) reason for disputing the violation; 
109 (ii) full legal name and address of the registered owner of the motor vehicle; and (iii) full legal 
110 name and address of the operator of the motor vehicle at the time the violation occurred. The 
111 registered owner may include signed statements from witnesses, including the names and 
112 addresses of witnesses, supporting the registered owner’s defense. Not more than 21 days after 
113 receipt of the signed affidavit, the enforcing authority or the hearing officer shall send the 
114 decision of the hearing officer, including the reasons for the outcome, by first class mail to the 
115 registered owner. If the registered owner is found responsible for the violation, the registered 
116 owner shall pay the fine in the manner described in paragraph (1) not more than 14 days after the 
117 issuance of the decision or request further judicial review pursuant to section 14 of chapter 30A. 
118 (3) In lieu of contesting responsibility for a violation in writing or online pursuant to 
119 paragraph (2) and not more than 60 days after a violation under this chapter, a registered owner 
120 of the motor vehicle may request a hearing to contest responsibility for a camera enforceable 
 
8 of 12 
121 violation. A hearing request shall be made in writing by mail or online. Upon receipt of a hearing 
122 request, the enforcing authority shall schedule the matter before a hearing officer. The hearing 
123 officer may be an employee of the enforcing authority or such other person as the enforcing 
124 authority may designate. Written notice of the date, time and place of the hearing shall be sent by 
125 first class mail to each registered owner of the motor vehicle. The hearing shall be informal, the 
126 rules of evidence shall not apply and the decision of the hearing officer shall be final subject to 
127 judicial review pursuant to section 14 of chapter 30A. Not more than 21 days after the hearing, 
128 the enforcing authority or the hearing officer shall send the decision of the hearing officer, 
129 including the reason for the outcome, by first class mail to the registered owner. If the registered 
130 owner is found to be responsible for the camera enforceable violation, the registered owner shall 
131 pay the fine in the manner described in this subsection not more than 14 days after the issuance 
132 of the decision or request further judicial review pursuant to said section 14 of said chapter 30A. 
133 (j) When a registered owner of a motor vehicle accrues not less than 3 unresolved 
134 violation notices from an enforcing authority pursuant to this chapter, such enforcing authority 
135 shall notify the registrar who shall not issue or renew the registered owner’s motor vehicle 
136 registration until after notification from the enforcing authority from whom the registrar received 
137 notification that all fines owed pursuant to this chapter have been paid. 
138 Section 3. (a) Notwithstanding section 2, if the registered owner of a motor vehicle is a 
139 person or entity engaged in the business of leasing or renting motor vehicles, and the motor 
140 vehicle was operated under a rental or lease agreement at the time of the camera enforceable 
141 violation, this section shall be applicable and the registered owner shall not be liable for any 
142 unpaid fines, except as provided in this section, if the registered owner has complied with the 
143 requirements of this section.

145 violation to the registered owner of a motor vehicle if a motor vehicle owned by the registered 
146 owner is involved in a camera enforceable violation. 
147 (c) Not more than 45 days after the violation, the registered owner shall furnish to the 
148 enforcing authority, in writing, the name and address of the lessee or rentee of the motor vehicle 
149 at the time of the camera enforceable violation, the lessee’s or rentee’s driver’s license number,

151 (d) Upon receipt of the information required under subsection (c), the enforcing authority 
152 shall issue a notice of a camera enforceable violation to the lessee or rentee in the form 
153 prescribed by section 2, and the lessee or rentee shall be liable for the violation. 
154 (e) An enforcing authority shall notify the registrar if a lessee or rentee has accrued not 
155 less than 5 unresolved violation notices with the enforcing authority pursuant to this chapter.

160 lessee or rentee pursuant to this chapter have been paid.

168 Section 5. (a) A photograph or other recorded evidence taken pursuant to this chapter 
169 shall not be discoverable in any judicial or administrative proceeding, other than a proceeding 
170 held pursuant to this chapter, without a court order. A photograph or other recorded evidence 
171 taken pursuant to this chapter shall not be admissible in any judicial or administrative 
172 proceeding, other than in a proceeding to adjudicate liability for a violation of this chapter, 
173 without a court order. A court shall not order a release of a photograph or other recorded 
174 evidence taken pursuant to this chapter unless the photograph or other recorded evidence 
175 establishes or undermines a finding of a camera enforceable violation and the camera 
176 enforceable violation is material as to a finding of civil or criminal liability. 
177 (b) Photographs and other recorded data collected by an enforcing authority pursuant to 
178 this chapter shall not be a public record under clause Twenty-sixth of section 7 of chapter 4 or 
179 chapter 66. 
180 (c) Photographs produced by a bus mounted or bus stop camera system shall not be used 
181 by an enforcing authority to identify the vehicle operator, the passengers or the contents of the 
182 vehicle. If a photograph taken pursuant to this chapter captures the vehicle operator, the 
183 passengers or the contents of the vehicle, the enforcing authority shall redact the photograph to 
184 remove or obscure the vehicle operator, passengers or contents of the vehicle before issuing a 
185 notice of violation under this chapter. An un-redacted photograph that includes the vehicle 
186 operator, passengers or contents of a motor vehicle committing a camera enforceable violation 
187 taken by a bus mounted or bus stop camera system shall not be discoverable or admissible in any

188 judicial or administrative proceeding except by the registered owner of the vehicle and shall not

194 Section 6. An enforcing authority may only recover costs reasonably related to the 
195 implementation and operation of any bus mounted or bus stop camera system, including, but not 
196 limited to, costs associated with: (i) installing, maintaining and operating the bus mounted safety 
197 camera system; (ii) issuing notices of camera enforceable violations; (iii) holding hearings for

199 under this chapter; and (v) collecting a fine; provided, however, that net revenues collected by an 
200 enforcing authority pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Massachusetts 
201 Transportation Trust Fund established in section 4 of chapter 6C. 
202 Section 7. The operation of emergency vehicles shall be subject to this chapter except 
203 that a driver of fire apparatus while going to a fire or responding to an alarm, the driver of a 
204 vehicle of a police or recognized protective department or the driver of an ambulance, in an 
205 emergency and while in performance of a public duty or while transporting a sick or injured 
206 person to a hospital or other destination where professional medical services are available, shall 
207 not be liable for a camera enforceable violation pursuant to this chapter.
