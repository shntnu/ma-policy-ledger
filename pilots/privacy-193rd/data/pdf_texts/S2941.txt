SENATE, August 29, 2024. 
The committee on Senate Ways and Means to whom was referred the House Bill to 
rename the Massachusetts Rehabilitation Commission, MassAbility, to reflect the self-
determination and limitless possibilities of people with disabilities (House, No. 4528); reports, 
recommending that the same ought to pass with an amendment striking out all after the enacting 
clause and inserting in place thereof the text of Senate document numbered 2941. 
 
For the committee, 
 Michael J. Rodrigues
