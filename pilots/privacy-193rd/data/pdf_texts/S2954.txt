An Act to provide for competitiveness and infrastructure investment in Massachusetts. 
 
 Whereas, The deferred operation of this act would tend to defeat its purpose, which is to 
enhance the competitiveness of Massachusetts in winning federal funds and to provide for the 
infrastructure needs of the Commonwealth, each of which is immediately necessary to 
accomplish important public purposes, therefore it is hereby declared to be an emergency law, 
necessary for the immediate preservation of the public convenience. 
 
 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives in General Court assembled, and by the authority 
of the same, as follows:
